Know what your CRM can observe.

Approved browser activity

ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Microsoft Copilot, and an explicit internal HTTPS domain. Observations begin after consent, while the local CRM is running.

Hashes, not raw content

Prompt fingerprints, selected-file hashes up to 10 MB, names and risk signals. A selected file or submit intent is not proof of successful upload. Exact hashes do not trace transformed images or internal provider data use.

Alerts where you work

Dashboard alerts, native notifications where supported, and an authenticated ntfy phone topic you configure. No provider passwords, historical chat import, SMS, or silent monitoring of other apps.
