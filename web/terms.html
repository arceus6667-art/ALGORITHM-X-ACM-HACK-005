Demo terms & privacy information

Version 2026-10-07-v2 · Effective 7 October 2026

1. What the demo provides

AgentTrap is a product demonstration for AI privacy, asset integrity, and policy governance. It provides browser-local document hashing, configured text-pattern detection, illustrative risk and confidence scoring, policy evaluation, simulated human review, and audit evidence export.

2. Trial access

Guest access lasts five minutes from the first trial start in the current browser. A verified Supabase account can start a separate thirty-minute trial. Refreshing, reopening the page, or signing in again does not reset an existing trial. Time continues while the page is closed or inactive. No payment or automatic subscription is attached to these trials.

At expiry, new demo actions stop. You can export evidence already created in the current page session. Guest identity uses a secure browser cookie. Signed-in timing is associated with a Supabase account identifier.

3. Documents and local data

Documents and extracted text are processed in your browser and are not uploaded by the demo. Use synthetic data or files you are authorized to analyze. File size is limited to 10 MB. TXT, Markdown, CSV, JSON, and log files support direct text inspection. For PDFs, Office documents, and images, exact byte hashing works, but content inspection requires pasted extracted text.

When signed in, asset metadata (including names, source references, fingerprints, classifications and decisions), policies, settings, approvals, review reasons and audit records are saved in your account-private Supabase workspace. Document bytes and pasted analysis text are not uploaded. Use non-sensitive labels and source references. Guest metadata is temporarily retained in session storage in this browser tab and can be imported into a new signed-in workspace. Export guest records before closing the tab. Existing saved account records take precedence over guest drafts.

4. Trial and hosting information

The server stores the trial's anonymous browser or Supabase account identifier, trial mode, start time, expiry time, and the version of these terms acknowledged at trial start. It does not store document bytes or document text. Supabase handles authentication, including account email or phone number, verification codes, provider identities and session tokens. Google handles its consent flow when enabled; SMS delivery uses the configured provider when enabled. Browser session tokens persist for sign-in continuity. This website does not collect a Google password. Workspace metadata is retained until the operator deletes your account or the service is retired; no automatic retention period is currently configured. Contact the site operator to request account and CRM record deletion.

Hosting receives ordinary requests needed to deliver the website, including standard network request information. A browser preference stores your selected theme. The guest cookie supports the trial timer. The site does not request microphone, camera, or location permissions.

5. Understanding results

Risk scores and confidence levels are prototype estimates, not calibrated probabilities, proof of a data transfer, or guarantees of safety. Decisions reflect configured conditions and user-supplied context. A BLOCK result demonstrates a policy outcome; it does not block activity on an external AI provider. Approval actions simulate review and do not grant real organizational permissions.

SHA-256 compares exact file bytes. Different bytes produce different fingerprints, including legitimate edits, resizing, and format conversion. A matching hash does not establish ownership or authorization. Hash chaining helps identify changed audit records relative to trusted evidence; it is not a digital signature or independent proof of authenticity.

6. Responsible use

Only analyze files you have permission to use. Do not attempt to bypass trial access, impersonate users, submit another person's credentials, disrupt the service, or use a prototype decision as your sole basis for a business-critical security action. Review all explanations and evidence before drawing conclusions.

7. Enterprise demo and availability

The enterprise option opens the same timed demonstration. It does not sell or install an enterprise security product. Prices shown in product documentation are proposed roadmap prices, not a checkout offer. Installed monitoring, SSO, organizational RBAC, signing-key management, and provider enforcement require a production deployment.

The demonstration is provided for evaluation and may be updated or unavailable. No security, compliance, or availability guarantee is offered. These demo terms describe the current preview and do not replace a production service agreement.
